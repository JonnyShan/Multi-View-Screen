Rider briefing

How to ride

Lean

Hold left or right to lean into a corner. Let go and the bike stands up and runs straight.

←→ADStick

Gas & brake

Hold forward or GAS to accelerate. Let go to roll off. Brake hard before the corner: braking mid-corner costs grip.

↑WRT gas ↓SSpaceLT brake

Line

Go wide on entry, clip the apex, then use every centimetre on the way out. Grass and gravel slow you down.
